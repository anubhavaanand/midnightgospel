How should the Floating 3D Text behave physically?

Since we are using physical 3D geometry for the words, how should they move through the scene?

A

Orbital Rings

Sentences form physical rings that orbit around the central entity. The speed of rotation and the color of the text are directly controlled by the mood JSON.

B

Physics Droplets

Individual words drop from the sky as rigid bodies using `@react-three/rapier`. They bounce off the floor and pile up around the player, creating a physical mountain of dialogue.

C

Kinetic Typography

Massive 3D words spawn in the foreground, scaling up and floating rapidly past the camera based on intensity, creating a highly cinematic, space-filling effect.
