# THE MIDNIGHT GOSPEL: 3D ASSET REQUIREMENTS BLUEPRINT

This document provides a highly structured checklist and visual reference guide for all 3D assets, meshes, color systems, and reference images required to construct the 8-Level WebGL Multiverse Landing Page.

## 🎨 GLOBAL HUB: CHROMATIC RIBBON (Clancy’s Home)
* **Vibe:** Escher-like, physics-free trailer park floating on a membranous ribbon in a colorful void.
* **Palette:** Electric magenta (`#FF3FAF`), warm coral (`#FF7A3C`), deep indigo void (`#1A1040`), acid yellow (`#FFE94A`).
* **3D Assets Checklist:**
  - [ ] **Möbius-Strip Ribbon:** A looping, twisting membranous landscape band in space.
  - [ ] **Clancy's Trailer:** Rounded-rectangle caravan with a giant, exaggerated satellite dish and loose cables.
  - [ ] **VR Simulator Pod:** Organic, yonic bio-computer VR helmet/pod with pulsing tubes and translucent flesh textures.
  - [ ] **Charlotte the Dog:** Fluffy white dog silhouette (no visible limbs) with glowing golden eyes and a black hole throat distortion particle effect.
  - [ ] **Resource Orbs & Rose Shrine:** Ambient floating props.

* **Vibe:** Chaotic urban collapse covered in zombie outbreaks under a toxic bubblegum pink sky.

* **3D Assets Checklist:**
  - [ ] **Beach Body Clancy (Avatar):** A muscular, buff purple humanoid version of Clancy.
