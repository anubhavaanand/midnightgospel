# Midnight Gospel 3D: Complete Research & Thematic Guide

## Design Insights from Liam Cobb (Background Design Lead)

### Visual Design Philosophy
- **House Style Rules** (enforced consistency across all episodes):
  - Every graphic element has smooth straight lines
  - All shapes and corners have the same diameter
  - Complex backgrounds must maintain "logic" and realism
  - Characters should be able to navigate spaces naturally without awkwardness

- **Artistic Influences**:
  - Hieronymus Bosch (twisted, apocalyptic landscapes)
  - Adult Swim animation
  - Æon Flux
  - Liquid Television
  - Watership Down
  - Mid-century architectural illustration

### Background Design Process
1. **Storyboard & Animatic Phase**: Concept established
2. **Environment Illustration**: Designers add details and expand concepts
3. **Layering in Photoshop**: Mark and group each layer for colorists/animators
4. **Metaphorical Mapping**: Backgrounds mirror dialogue topics and emotional themes

### Key Visual Principles
- **Wackiness with Realism**: Surreal yet explorable environments
- **Background as Narrative**: Settings actively comment on themes being discussed
- **Symbolic Landscapes**: 
  - Soul Prison (Episode 5): Skeletons crawling from earth, Bosch-inspired
  - Meat City (Episode 2): Industrial nightmares reflecting death/acceptance themes
  - Zombie White House (Episode 1): Chaotic, malfunctioning simulation aesthetic

## 3D Adaptation Guidance from Research

### Visual Consistency Rules for 3D Implementation
1. **Geometry**: Use consistent line weights and corner radii (via Sobel-filter post-processing)
2. **Spacing**: Maintain architectural logic—spaces must feel navigable
3. **Texture**: Balance wackiness with realism (exaggeration should feel grounded)
4. **Color**: Strict palette adherence with RGB separation for psychedelic moments
5. **Animation**: Characters move through spaces naturally (no awkward clipping)

### Symbolic Background Mapping for 3D Levels
- **Chromatic Void** (Level 0): Swirling Perlin noise, liquid membrane quality
- **Zombie White House** (Level 1): Modular architecture warping, decay animations
- **Meat Factory** (Level 2): Organic machinery, grinding displacement shaders
- **Underwater Ice World** (Level 3): Refraction shaders, non-Euclidean geometry tricks
- **Soul Prison** (Level 4): Bosch-inspired twisted earth, rim-lighting for atmosphere
- **The Exit** (Level 5): Particle explosion transitioning to white (consciousness expansion)

### Design Principles for AI-Generated Assets
When using Gemini 3 for procedural generation:
1. **Enforce house style** via JSON prompts (smooth lines, consistent corners)
2. **Map colors** strictly to palette
3. **Ground surrealism** by ensuring logical spatial relationships
4. **Layer symbolism** (backgrounds should comment on dialogue/theme)
5. **Test navigation** (ensure characters/camera can move through generated spaces)

## Synthesis: Show as "Trojan Horse"

### Core Concept
The series lures audiences in with:
- **Bright colors & absurdity** (visual spectacle)
- **Chaotic, wacky worlds** (entertainment value)
- **Floating heads in pods** (humor, whimsy)

Then hits them with:
- **Profound existential truths**
- **Real grief and mortality**
- **Spiritual practice teachings**
- **Compassion & connection**

### 3D Adaptation Strategy
Apply this "Trojan horse" principle to interactive experience:
1. **Level 0-1**: Immerse in wackiness (visual dazzle, interactive toys)
2. **Level 2-3**: Introduce deeper metaphors (symbolic environments)
3. **Level 4-5**: Emotional climax (high-contrast lighting, existential themes)
4. **Exit**: Transcendence moment (particle explosion, white-out, reset)

Users think they're playing a "trippy game" but internalize profound ideas about death, connection, and consciousness.

## Technical Glossary for 3D Implementation

- **Yonic**: Vulva-shaped; refers to pod design (womb-like entry)
- **Domain Warping**: Perlin noise distortion creating organic flow
- **Vertex Shader Displacement**: Geometric deformation for organic motion
- **Glitch Artifact**: Abstract blocky structure simulating corrupted data
- **Bardo Loop**: Cyclical death-rebirth narrative framing
- **Ego Death**: Dissolution of self; voxel destruction mechanic
- **Attractor Gravity**: Planet-specific gravity wells pulling objects
- **Chromatic Aberration**: RGB channel separation for lens imperfection
- **Bloom Pass**: Neon glow/light scattering effect
- **Toon Shading**: "2D in 3D" outline rendering
